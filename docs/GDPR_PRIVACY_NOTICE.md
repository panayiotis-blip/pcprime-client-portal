## 1. Who we are

PC Prime & Calculate Consultants Ltd ("we", "us", "the firm") is the **data controller** for the personal data we process about you. We provide accounting, bookkeeping, tax, payroll and business-consultancy services, and we operate a secure client portal for exchanging documents and information.

If you have any questions about this notice or your data, contact us at **info@primeandcalculate.com** or **+357 24 258346**. [Our Data Protection contact is: _______________.]

## 2. The personal data we collect

Depending on the services we provide to you, we may process:

- **Identity details** — name, trading name, date of birth, nationality, ID or passport number.
- **Tax and statutory identifiers** — Tax Identification Code (TIC), VAT number, company registration number, employer/Ergani number, Social Insurance number.
- **Contact details** — address, phone, mobile, email, website.
- **Due-diligence (KYC/AML) information** — information we are legally required to collect to verify your identity and assess risk, including source-of-funds and beneficial-ownership details.
- **Financial and accounting records** — invoices, receipts, ledgers, tax returns and filings, payroll and billing information.
- **Documents you provide** — e.g. bank statements, invoices and statutory documents you upload or send us.
- **Correspondence** — emails, portal messages and records of calls with you.
- **Portal account data** — if you have a portal login, your account and access records.

We collect this information from you directly, from documents you provide, and (where relevant) from public registries and authorities.

## 3. Why we use your data, and our legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Providing our accounting, tax, payroll and consultancy services to you | Performance of our contract with you |
| Meeting our legal duties — accounting and tax record-keeping, statutory filings, and anti-money-laundering (KYC/AML) obligations | Legal obligation |
| Managing our relationship, billing and collections, and securing our systems | Our legitimate interests (running and protecting the firm), balanced against your rights |

We do **not** use your data for marketing without your consent, and we do **not** make automated decisions that produce legal effects about you.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Public authorities** — where required, e.g. the Cyprus Tax Department (TAXISnet), Social Insurance Services and the Registrar of Companies.
- **Service providers who process data on our behalf** ("processors"), under contract and only as needed to run the portal:
  - **Supabase** — secure database, storage and application backend (data hosted in the **EU**).
  - **Vercel** — hosting of the web application (**EU**, Frankfurt).
  - **Google Workspace / Gmail** — the firm's email.
  - **Sentry** — error monitoring (**EU**; personal data is scrubbed).
  - **Anthropic** — AI-assisted reading of documents you scan/upload (see international transfers below).

We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. International transfers

Most of your data stays within the **European Union**. One feature is an exception: when a document is scanned, we may use an AI service (**Anthropic**, based in the **United States**) to read and extract its details. This means the document image may be transferred to the US.

This transfer is protected by [**Standard Contractual Clauses** and a data-processing agreement with the provider], and the service is configured [not to retain the data or use it for training]. The firm can **disable** this AI feature entirely, in which case document reading is done locally and no data leaves EU infrastructure. If you would prefer we do not use the AI feature for your documents, please tell us.

## 6. How long we keep your data

We keep your accounting, tax and AML records for the period required by law — generally **around 6–7 years** in Cyprus [confirm] — after which we delete or anonymise your personal data. Operational and technical records (e.g. logs) are kept for shorter periods and deleted automatically. When we no longer need to keep your personal data, we anonymise your record so that the accounting entries we must retain no longer identify you.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
- ask us to **correct** inaccurate data;
- ask us to **erase** your data (subject to our legal duty to retain accounting/AML records);
- ask us to **restrict** or **object to** certain processing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- **withdraw consent** where we rely on it; and
- **complain** to the supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these, contact us at **info@primeandcalculate.com**. We will respond within one month. Please note some data must be retained to meet our legal obligations even if you ask us to erase it.

You can also complain to the Cypriot supervisory authority: **Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection**, Nicosia, Cyprus (commissioner.dataprotection.gov.cy).

## 8. How we protect your data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures, including access controls that keep each client's data separate, multi-factor authentication for staff, encryption of data in transit and at rest, encrypted storage of any credentials, activity audit logging, and regular security review.
